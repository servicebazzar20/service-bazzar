# Service Bazzar
Service Bazzar is a local business directory that helps users discover and connect with trusted service providers across multiple categories.
## About Service Bazzar
Service Bazzar makes it easier for customers to find relevant service providers for their personal and business needs.
## Service Categories
- Home Services
- Professional Services
- Business Services
- Health & Wellness
- Automotive Services
- Event Services
